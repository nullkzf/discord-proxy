Unofficial. Not affiliated with Discord Inc. Opens only Discord through a small local proxy; not a VPN. See the README.

**Windows (10 and 11):** download **`DiscordProxy-Setup-….exe`**, run it, then open **Discord Proxy** from the Start menu or desktop instead of the normal Discord icon.
No administrator rights needed. The installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
`DiscordProxy-…-portable.zip` is the same program without an installer.

**Mac (macOS 12 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel):** download **`DiscordProxy-…-mac.dmg`**, drag **Discord Proxy** into Applications, open it from there instead of the normal Discord.
The app is not notarized by Apple, so the first time macOS blocks it: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (details in the README).

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.
